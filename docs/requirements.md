# Requirements and their evidence

Each requirement the reader and the service meet, and the tests that prove it. The table is
checked by `tests/test_traceability.py`: every test named here must exist, every requirement must
name at least one test, and every test must prove at least one requirement. A change that adds a
test adds it here; a change that removes one finds out what it was proving.

Evidence is `file::test` under `tests/`. A test parametrized over corpus documents (the Word
oracle, the mutations) proves its requirement for every document in `corpus/`.
